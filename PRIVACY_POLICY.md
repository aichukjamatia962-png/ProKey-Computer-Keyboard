# ProKey – Computer Keyboard: Privacy Policy

Last updated: (set date before publishing)

ProKey is a keyboard. What you type stays on your device.

- ProKey does not collect, store, log or transmit anything you type, including passwords and one-time codes.
- ProKey has no INTERNET permission, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- The only data ProKey saves is your keyboard settings (theme, layout, sizes, sound and vibration options), stored locally on your device.
- ProKey requests the VIBRATE permission only to vibrate on key presses.
- This version has no clipboard history, word suggestions or personal dictionary. If a future version adds them, they will be stored only on your device, clipboard history will be off by default, and this policy will be updated before release.
- Uninstalling ProKey removes its settings.
